# Pillar 8: Email Marketing, Retention & Customer Experience
### Marketing Infrastructure Development — Implementation Blueprint

The business should not focus exclusively on acquiring new customers. It should also have systems for keeping existing customers engaged, encouraging them to purchase again, recovering customers who have stopped buying, and turning satisfied customers into advocates.

This pillar covers the communication and relationship infrastructure that supports customers before, during, and after a purchase.

It is broader than simply setting up email campaigns. It includes customer segmentation, lifecycle communication, retention strategies, loyalty programmes, customer feedback, post-purchase experiences, and customer relationship improvement.

## 1. Customer retention strategy and assessment
First, understand how the business currently maintains relationships with existing customers and identify opportunities to improve repeat purchases and customer loyalty.

### Tasks and activities
- Review the business's existing customer retention practices.
- Identify how the business communicates with customers after a purchase.
- Assess whether customer information and purchase history are properly recorded.
- Examine existing email, SMS, WhatsApp, and other customer communication activities.
- Analyze available historical customer and transaction data.
- Identify the percentage of customers who make repeat purchases, where data allows.
- Examine how frequently customers purchase.
- Identify products or services that encourage repeat purchases.
- Determine the average time between purchases.
- Identify customer drop-off and inactivity patterns.
- Assess existing loyalty, referral, membership, or rewards programmes.
- Identify reasons customers may stop purchasing.
- Establish retention objectives and relevant performance indicators.
- Develop a retention strategy appropriate to the business model.
### Key deliverables
- Customer retention audit.
- Existing customer communication assessment.
- Customer repeat-purchase analysis.
- Customer inactivity and churn assessment, where data allows.
- Retention opportunity report.
- Customer retention strategy.
- Retention KPI framework.
## 2. Customer database and contact management infrastructure
The business needs an organized customer database that allows it to understand who its customers are, what they have purchased, and how to communicate with them appropriately.

### Tasks and activities
- Audit existing customer databases and contact lists.
- Identify all customer data sources:
  - CRM records.
  - E-commerce transactions.
  - Website registrations.
  - Email subscriptions.
  - Lead and customer forms.
  - Previous inquiries.
  - Physical customer records.
  - Loyalty and membership records.
- Consolidate customer records where appropriate.
- Identify and resolve duplicate customer records.
- Establish a standardized customer data structure.
- Capture relevant information, including:
  - Customer name.
  - Email address.
  - Phone number.
  - Location.
  - Purchase history.
  - Products or services purchased.
  - Purchase frequency.
  - Total customer spend, where available.
  - Last purchase date.
  - Customer status.
  - Communication preferences.
  - Marketing consent.
- Separate prospects, first-time customers, repeat customers, and inactive customers.
- Establish procedures for updating customer information.
- Configure customer database access and permissions.
- Define customer data retention and deletion procedures.
- Ensure customer information is collected and processed appropriately.
### Key deliverables
- Centralized customer database.
- Customer data structure and field definitions.
- Customer record management procedures.
- Customer deduplication and data cleaning report.
- Customer data access and governance rules.
- Customer communication consent records, where supported.
## 3. Email marketing platform selection and infrastructure setup
The business needs a suitable email marketing system for storing subscribers, managing contact lists, designing campaigns, and delivering customer communications.

### Tasks and activities
- Assess the business's email marketing requirements.
- Determine whether the existing CRM or business platform has sufficient email marketing capabilities.
- Evaluate suitable email marketing platforms based on:
  - Contact database size.
  - Campaign volume.
  - Automation capabilities.
  - Segmentation features.
  - Integration options.
  - Reporting and analytics.
  - Deliverability features.
  - Pricing and scalability.
- Select the appropriate platform.
- Create and configure the email marketing account.
- Connect the platform to the website or e-commerce system.
- Connect the email platform to the CRM where necessary.
- Configure the business's sender name and sender email address.
- Configure domain authentication, including SPF, DKIM, and DMARC as appropriate.
- Establish appropriate sending domains and subdomains where necessary.
- Configure subscription forms and contact lists.
- Set up unsubscribe and preference management functionality.
- Configure email templates and branding.
- Test email delivery across different email providers.
- Establish procedures for managing subscribers and maintaining list quality.
### Key deliverables
- Email marketing platform recommendation.
- Configured email marketing account.
- Connected customer and subscriber databases.
- Email sender configuration.
- Domain authentication configuration.
- Branded email templates.
- Subscription and unsubscribe infrastructure.
- Email platform setup and testing report.
## 4. Customer segmentation and lifecycle classification
Customers should not all receive the same communication. Their needs, interests, purchasing behaviour, and relationship with the business differ.

### Tasks and activities
- Define the customer segments relevant to the business.
- Segment customers based on demographics and relevant characteristics.
- Segment customers by products or services purchased.
- Group customers by purchase frequency.
- Group customers by total spending or commercial value.
- Identify first-time customers.
- Identify repeat customers.
- Identify high-value customers.
- Identify customers who purchase specific product categories.
- Identify customers who have not purchased within a defined period.
- Identify customers who have abandoned purchases or shown buying interest without completing a transaction, where tracking supports this.
- Segment customers according to email engagement.
- Identify customers who regularly open, click, or interact with communications, while accounting for email privacy-related measurement limitations.
- Develop customer lifecycle categories:
  - New subscriber.
  - New customer.
  - Active customer.
  - Repeat customer.
  - Loyal customer.
  - Inactive customer.
  - At-risk customer.
  - Reactivated customer.
- Configure corresponding segments in the CRM or email platform.
- Define the communication approach for each segment.
### Key deliverables
- Customer segmentation framework.
- Customer lifecycle classification model.
- Configured customer segments.
- Customer value and purchase behaviour segments.
- Customer communication segmentation rules.
## 5. Customer journey and lifecycle communication mapping
The business should have a communication plan for the different stages of the customer relationship, rather than contacting customers only when it wants to sell something.

### Tasks and activities
- Map the customer journey from initial subscription or purchase through repeat purchases and long-term engagement.
- Identify the communication needs at each stage.
- Define what customers should receive after:
  - Subscribing to the mailing list.
  - Creating an account.
  - Making their first purchase.
  - Completing a payment.
  - Receiving a product or service.
  - Requesting assistance.
  - Making a repeat purchase.
  - Becoming inactive.
  - Reaching a loyalty milestone.
- Identify appropriate communication channels for each stage.
- Define the purpose of each communication.
- Determine the information customers need at each stage.
- Establish communication frequency.
- Identify opportunities for upselling and cross-selling.
- Define when customers should transition between lifecycle stages.
- Map the customer journey across website, email, messaging, and other relevant touchpoints.
- Identify communication gaps and points of customer frustration.
### Key deliverables
- Customer journey map.
- Customer lifecycle communication framework.
- Customer touchpoint inventory.
- Lifecycle messaging plan.
- Customer communication gap analysis.
## 6. Email campaign strategy and planning
Develop a structured approach to email campaigns based on customer needs, business objectives, and the different stages of the customer lifecycle.

### Tasks and activities
- Define the objectives of email marketing.
- Identify the types of campaigns the business needs.
- Develop a campaign strategy for:
  - Promotional campaigns.
  - New product or service announcements.
  - Product launches.
  - Educational newsletters.
  - Seasonal campaigns.
  - Special offers.
  - Product recommendations.
  - Customer reactivation.
  - Loyalty and exclusive customer campaigns.
  - Event or important business announcements.
- Determine which customer segments should receive each campaign.
- Define campaign frequency and scheduling.
- Establish campaign themes and content topics.
- Identify the offers and calls to action for each campaign.
- Develop a campaign calendar.
- Establish campaign approval and review procedures.
- Plan campaign testing and performance evaluation.
### Key deliverables
- Email marketing strategy.
- Email campaign categories and objectives.
- Customer segment-to-campaign mapping.
- Email campaign calendar.
- Campaign content plan.
- Email campaign performance framework.
## 7. Email content creation and campaign design
The business needs professionally structured emails that communicate clearly, reflect the brand, and encourage the intended customer action.

### Tasks and activities
- Develop branded email design templates.
- Establish consistent email layouts and formatting.
- Create promotional email designs.
- Create educational and informational email formats.
- Develop newsletter templates.
- Write email subject lines and preview text.
- Create persuasive email copy.
- Develop appropriate calls to action.
- Incorporate relevant product images and supporting graphics.
- Include testimonials, reviews, or customer stories where available and authorized.
- Personalize content using customer information where appropriate.
- Ensure emails display correctly on mobile devices.
- Check accessibility, readability, and image loading.
- Test links, buttons, product information, and promotional codes.
- Review emails for spelling, accuracy, and brand consistency.
- Establish a content approval process before campaigns are sent.
### Key deliverables
- Branded email design system.
- Promotional email templates.
- Newsletter and educational email templates.
- Email copy and subject line library.
- Email content production guidelines.
- Mobile-responsive email templates.
- Email quality assurance checklist.
## 8. Email automation and lifecycle campaigns
Beyond manually sending campaigns, the business should establish relevant automated communications triggered by customer actions, subscriptions, purchases, and lifecycle changes.

### Tasks and activities
- Identify customer interactions that should trigger automated emails.
- Configure a welcome email sequence for new subscribers.
- Configure welcome or onboarding emails for new customers.
- Configure order confirmation and transaction-related messages where the platform supports them.
- Configure payment, shipping, delivery, or service-status notifications where applicable.
- Develop post-purchase communication sequences.
- Configure product education and usage guidance emails.
- Develop abandoned cart or checkout recovery sequences where supported.
- Configure replenishment reminders for products customers regularly need to repurchase.
- Develop cross-sell and upsell email sequences.
- Configure birthday, anniversary, or milestone emails where appropriate and based on reliable data.
- Develop win-back sequences for inactive customers.
- Configure loyalty and rewards notifications.
- Establish appropriate delays between automated messages.
- Configure entry and exit conditions for automation workflows.
- Establish suppression rules to prevent customers from receiving irrelevant or conflicting messages.
- Test all automated sequences before activation.
- Monitor delivery, engagement, and conversion performance.
### Key deliverables
- Customer email automation strategy.
- Welcome email sequence.
- New customer onboarding sequence.
- Post-purchase email sequence.
- Abandoned cart recovery sequence, where applicable.
- Cross-sell and upsell sequences.
- Replenishment and reminder sequences, where applicable.
- Customer win-back sequence.
- Configured automation workflows.
- Automation testing documentation.
Important distinction: Email marketing automation is part of the customer communication infrastructure here. More advanced cross-platform workflows involving several systems and complex business processes are covered in Pillar 10.
## 9. Customer retention and repeat-purchase programmes
The business needs deliberate strategies that give customers reasons to return and continue buying.

### Tasks and activities
- Identify the factors that encourage customers to purchase repeatedly.
- Determine which products and services naturally support repeat purchases.
- Identify opportunities to introduce complementary products or services.
- Develop repeat-purchase campaigns.
- Establish relevant replenishment or reorder reminders.
- Design customer loyalty programmes where commercially appropriate.
- Develop points-based or rewards-based programmes where suitable.
- Establish membership or subscription programmes where relevant.
- Create exclusive offers for returning customers.
- Develop VIP or high-value customer engagement programmes.
- Design referral programmes that encourage satisfied customers to recommend the business.
- Establish customer appreciation campaigns.
- Identify opportunities for personalized product recommendations.
- Evaluate the cost and commercial viability of retention incentives.
- Measure repeat-purchase rates and revenue generated by retention programmes.
### Key deliverables
- Customer retention programme strategy.
- Repeat-purchase campaign framework.
- Customer loyalty programme structure.
- VIP customer engagement framework.
- Referral programme structure.
- Replenishment and reorder strategy.
- Customer appreciation campaign plan.
- Retention programme performance framework.
## 10. Customer experience and post-purchase management
Customer experience is the overall impression customers form through their interactions with the business. It extends beyond the quality of the product itself.

The objective is to make the experience consistent, convenient, trustworthy, and satisfactory across relevant touchpoints.

### Tasks and activities
- Map the customer experience from purchase to post-purchase.
- Review the customer's experience after payment or order confirmation.
- Assess the clarity of order, delivery, booking, and service information.
- Review delivery or service fulfillment communication.
- Assess the ease of contacting the business.
- Review customer onboarding and product or service instructions.
- Identify common customer frustrations and unnecessary steps.
- Review how the business handles complaints and customer concerns.
- Establish customer communication standards.
- Develop post-purchase guidance and support information.
- Establish procedures for handling delayed orders, cancellations, refunds, and service issues.
- Define customer support escalation procedures.
- Identify opportunities to improve convenience and customer satisfaction.
- Ensure customer interactions are documented in the appropriate system.
- Review the experience across website, email, WhatsApp, phone, and physical touchpoints where applicable.
### Key deliverables
- Customer experience audit.
- Customer journey experience map.
- Post-purchase communication framework.
- Customer service communication standards.
- Customer complaint and escalation procedures.
- Customer experience improvement recommendations.
## 11. Customer feedback, reviews and reputation management
Customer feedback helps the business understand what customers appreciate, what frustrates them, and what needs to change.

### Tasks and activities
- Identify suitable methods for collecting customer feedback.
- Develop post-purchase surveys.
- Create customer satisfaction questionnaires.
- Establish customer review and testimonial collection procedures.
- Configure review requests at appropriate points in the customer journey.
- Monitor feedback received through email, social media, WhatsApp, and other relevant channels.
- Identify recurring complaints and customer concerns.
- Categorize feedback by product, service, delivery, support, or other relevant factors.
- Establish procedures for responding to negative feedback.
- Create processes for escalating serious complaints.
- Identify opportunities to convert positive feedback into testimonials and case studies with customer permission.
- Monitor relevant public review platforms.
- Establish procedures for responding to reviews professionally.
- Share recurring customer feedback with relevant business teams.
- Use feedback to improve products, services, marketing messages, and customer experience.
### Key deliverables
- Customer feedback collection strategy.
- Customer satisfaction survey templates.
- Review and testimonial collection process.
- Customer feedback classification framework.
- Review response guidelines.
- Customer complaint analysis.
- Customer experience improvement report.
## 12. Customer reactivation and win-back strategy
Some customers eventually stop purchasing or engaging with the business. The business should have a structured approach to identifying and re-engaging these customers.

### Tasks and activities
- Define what qualifies as an inactive customer.
- Determine inactivity periods based on the normal purchasing cycle of the business.
- Identify customers who have not purchased within the expected period.
- Segment inactive customers by previous purchasing behaviour and value.
- Identify possible reasons for customer inactivity using available information.
- Develop reactivation campaigns for suitable customer segments.
- Create personalized win-back messages.
- Consider relevant incentives where commercially justified.
- Develop product or service update campaigns for inactive customers.
- Configure reactivation email sequences.
- Use approved communication channels where appropriate.
- Establish procedures for handling customers who respond to reactivation campaigns.
- Identify customers who should be excluded from further marketing communication.
- Measure reactivation rates and revenue recovered.
### Key deliverables
- Customer inactivity classification framework.
- Customer reactivation strategy.
- Win-back campaign templates.
- Configured reactivation workflows.
- Customer reactivation reporting.
- Inactive customer management procedures.
## 13. Customer personalization and experience optimization
Customers should receive relevant communication and experiences based on their interests, preferences, and relationship with the business.

### Tasks and activities
- Identify customer information that can support personalization.
- Personalize email content using customer names and relevant information.
- Recommend products or services based on previous purchases where appropriate.
- Create customer-specific offers where commercially justified.
- Customize communication based on customer lifecycle stage.
- Develop segment-specific product recommendations.
- Identify opportunities for personalized website or landing page experiences.
- Review whether personalization improves engagement and conversion.
- Avoid irrelevant or excessive personalization.
- Ensure customer data is used appropriately and securely.
- Test different communication approaches and offers across customer segments.
### Key deliverables
- Customer personalization strategy.
- Personalized email content framework.
- Product recommendation strategy.
- Segment-specific communication templates.
- Personalization testing plan.
- Customer experience optimization recommendations.
## 14. Customer retention and email performance measurement
The business needs to measure whether its customer communication and retention activities actually improve engagement, repeat purchases, and revenue.

### Tasks and activities
- Define email marketing KPIs.
- Track email delivery rates.
- Monitor bounce rates and spam complaints.
- Measure open rates while accounting for privacy-related measurement limitations.
- Track click-through rates.
- Measure email conversion rates where properly tracked.
- Track unsubscribe rates.
- Measure revenue generated by email campaigns where attribution is sufficiently reliable.
- Track repeat-purchase rates.
- Measure customer retention and churn using appropriate definitions.
- Monitor customer lifetime value where sufficient data exists.
- Measure loyalty and referral programme performance.
- Track customer satisfaction and feedback trends.
- Measure reactivation rates.
- Compare performance across customer segments.
- Identify campaigns and sequences producing the strongest results.
- Identify communication fatigue and declining engagement.
- Build email and retention dashboards.
- Establish regular reporting and optimization reviews.
### Key deliverables
- Email marketing KPI framework.
- Email campaign performance dashboard.
- Email automation performance reports.
- Customer retention dashboard.
- Repeat-purchase and customer lifetime value reports, where data allows.
- Customer satisfaction reporting.
- Customer reactivation reports.
- Retention performance review and recommendations.
## 15. Customer communication governance and continuous improvement
Customer communication must remain organized, consistent, relevant, and compliant as the business grows.

### Tasks and activities
- Establish customer communication guidelines.
- Define approved email and messaging channels.
- Establish rules for campaign frequency.
- Define communication preferences and consent management procedures.
- Configure unsubscribe and suppression lists.
- Establish procedures for handling bounced or invalid email addresses.
- Define ownership of customer communication activities.
- Establish email campaign approval procedures.
- Review email deliverability and domain reputation.
- Audit automated sequences periodically.
- Review customer feedback and retention performance.
- Identify outdated messages, broken links, and irrelevant offers.
- Update email templates and communication sequences.
- Review customer data quality.
- Establish a recurring customer experience and retention improvement process.
### Key deliverables
- Customer communication policy.
- Email marketing governance procedures.
- Consent and unsubscribe management framework.
- Email deliverability monitoring process.
- Campaign approval and quality assurance procedures.
- Customer communication review schedule.
- Retention and customer experience improvement plan.

- An organized customer database.
- A functioning email marketing infrastructure.
- Properly segmented customer lists.
- A documented customer journey and lifecycle communication strategy.
- Structured email campaigns and automated customer communication.
- Repeat-purchase, loyalty, referral, and reactivation strategies.
- A defined post-purchase customer experience.
- Customer feedback and review collection systems.
- Customer retention and email performance reporting.
- Procedures for continuously improving customer relationships.
