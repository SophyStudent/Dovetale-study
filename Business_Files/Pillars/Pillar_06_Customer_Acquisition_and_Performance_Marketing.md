# Pillar 6: Customer Acquisition & Performance Marketing
### Marketing Infrastructure Development — Implementation Blueprint

Pillar 5 established how to convert website visitors into leads, inquiries, bookings, and customers.

Pillar 6 focuses on getting the right people to those conversion points and managing the campaigns that bring them there.

This goes beyond simply creating advertisements and boosting social media posts. It involves developing an acquisition strategy, selecting the right advertising platforms, configuring advertising accounts, researching audiences, building campaigns, managing budgets, developing creatives, implementing campaign tracking, and continuously improving advertising performance.

The goal is to ensure that the business knows where its customers come from, how much it costs to acquire them, which campaigns produce valuable results, and how effectively its marketing budget is being spent.

- Identify the primary acquisition objectives:
  - Brand awareness.
  - Website traffic.
  - Lead generation.
  - Product purchases.
  - Service inquiries.
  - Appointment bookings.
  - App installations, where relevant.
  - Customer acquisition.
- Identify the most commercially valuable audience segments.
- Determine the most appropriate acquisition channels.
- Evaluate the suitability of paid advertising platforms for the business.
- Identify the customer journey from advertisement to conversion.
- Determine the offers and messages to promote.
- Establish acquisition targets.
- Estimate expected acquisition costs using available historical data or clearly stated assumptions.
- Identify seasonal acquisition opportunities.
- Establish the overall advertising strategy and campaign priorities.

## 9. Campaign tracking and advertising platform measurement
The business must be able to understand what happens after someone interacts with an advertisement.

This section establishes the campaign-level tracking requirements and verifies that advertising platforms receive the relevant conversion signals.

The complete analytics architecture, cross-platform tracking governance, attribution, and reporting will be developed in greater depth under Pillar 9.

### Tasks and activities
- Identify the website or app actions that matter to each campaign.
- Define the conversion events relevant to the advertising objectives.
- Identify which events should be reported to each advertising platform.
- Review existing tracking implementations before installing anything new.
- Configure or verify Meta Pixel where applicable.
- Configure or verify Meta Conversions API where appropriate.
- Configure or verify Google Ads conversion tracking.
- Configure other platform-specific conversion tracking where required.
- Identify whether native platform integrations, Google Tag Manager, direct code installation, or server-side implementations are appropriate.
- Verify that the correct website or app properties are connected to advertising accounts.
- Confirm that the correct conversion events are selected for campaign optimization.
- Test important events such as:
  - PageView.
  - ViewContent.
  - Lead.
  - AddToCart.
  - InitiateCheckout.
  - Purchase.
  - CompleteRegistration.
  - Booking or appointment completion, where relevant.
- Verify event parameters and values where applicable.
- Check that purchase values and currencies are passed correctly.
- Test that events are received by the appropriate advertising platforms.
- Identify missing or duplicated conversion events.
- Review browser and server event deduplication where applicable.
- Confirm that campaign landing pages preserve relevant tracking parameters.
- Verify UTM tagging conventions.
- Document tracking dependencies and unresolved issues.
### Key deliverables
- Campaign conversion tracking requirements.
- Advertising pixel and tag configuration or verification.
- Platform conversion event configuration.
- Conversion tracking test report.
- UTM campaign tagging framework.
- Advertising account-to-website connection documentation.
- Tracking issue and implementation report.
Important: Do not install a second pixel or duplicate conversion tag simply because a new campaign is being launched. First inspect the existing implementation. For example, a Shopify store may already send Meta events through its native integration. Additional implementations must be coordinated to prevent duplicate events.

### Tasks and activities

- Create product and service page visitor audiences.
- Create Add to Cart audiences where relevant.
- Create checkout abandonment audiences where relevant.
- Create social media engagement audiences.
- Create video viewer audiences where supported.
- Create lead form interaction audiences where available.
- Identify existing customer audiences for relevant cross-selling and upselling campaigns.
- Exclude people who have already completed the desired conversion where appropriate.
- Establish audience membership durations.
- Segment audiences according to their previous interactions.
- Develop suitable retargeting messages for each segment.
- Create different creatives for different levels of purchase intent.
- Develop cart and checkout recovery campaigns where supported.
- Coordinate retargeting with email and CRM follow-up systems.
- Establish appropriate frequency controls where available.
- Monitor audience size and delivery limitations.
- Review retargeting performance.
### Key deliverables
- Retargeting strategy.
- Website and social engagement audiences.
- Cart and checkout abandonment audiences where applicable.
- Retargeting campaign structures.
- Audience exclusion rules.
- Retargeting creative and messaging library.
- Retargeting performance reports.

## 12. Campaign monitoring and performance optimization
Launching a campaign is not the end of the work. Campaigns need regular monitoring to identify problems, improve efficiency, and increase the quality of results.

### Tasks and activities
- Monitor campaign delivery and spending.
- Review impressions and reach.
- Monitor frequency.
- Review clicks and click-through rates (CTR).
- Monitor cost per click (CPC).
- Review cost per thousand impressions (CPM).
- Monitor landing page views.
- Review leads, inquiries, bookings, and purchases.
- Monitor cost per lead (CPL).
- Assess customer acquisition cost (CAC) where measurable.
- Review ROAS where revenue attribution is sufficiently reliable.
- Compare performance across advertising platforms.
- Compare performance across campaigns and ad sets.
- Compare audience segment performance.
- Compare creative and messaging performance.
- Identify advertising fatigue.
- Identify poor-quality traffic and leads.
- Investigate campaigns with unusually high costs.
- Review the relationship between ad clicks and landing page activity.
- Identify discrepancies between advertising platform reports and website analytics.
- Pause or revise underperforming campaigns when justified.
- Test new creative variations.
- Test alternative audience structures.
- Adjust bids, budgets, and placements where appropriate.
- Document optimization decisions and results.
### Key deliverables
- Campaign performance dashboard.
- Advertising performance reports.
- Audience performance analysis.
- Creative performance analysis.
- Campaign optimization records.
- Advertising efficiency assessment.
- Performance improvement recommendations.
## 13. Lead quality and customer acquisition evaluation
Generating a large number of leads does not necessarily mean an advertising campaign is successful. The business needs to understand whether the leads are relevant, qualified, and likely to become paying customers.

### Tasks and activities
- Establish criteria for identifying qualified leads.
- Review the quality of leads generated by each campaign.
- Compare lead quality across advertising platforms.
- Compare lead quality across audiences.
- Compare lead quality across creatives and offers.
- Identify campaigns generating irrelevant inquiries.
- Identify campaigns generating duplicate or fraudulent leads.
- Review the percentage of leads that progress to sales conversations.
- Review the percentage of leads that become customers.
- Compare acquisition costs against actual customer value.
- Identify the advertising sources responsible for valuable customers.
- Collect feedback from the sales or lead management process.
- Identify common reasons leads fail to convert.
- Feed lead quality information back into advertising targeting.
- Refine audience segments based on actual customer outcomes.
- Identify opportunities to improve lead forms and campaign messaging.
### Key deliverables
- Lead quality assessment.
- Campaign-to-customer acquisition analysis.
- Qualified lead performance report.
- Lead source effectiveness report.
- Advertising audience refinement recommendations.
- Customer acquisition efficiency assessment.

Important distinction: Pillar 6 versus Pillar 7
Pillar 6: Customer Acquisition & Performance Marketing	Pillar 7: Lead Management & Sales Enablement
Attracts potential customers.	Manages potential customers after they express interest.
Runs paid advertising campaigns.	Organizes and manages incoming leads.
Generates traffic and inquiries.	Qualifies and prioritizes leads.
Builds advertising audiences.	Maintains customer and prospect records.
Measures acquisition costs.	Measures lead progression and sales conversion.
Optimizes campaign performance.	Improves lead follow-up and sales processes.

For example, Pillar 6 generates 200 inquiries through Meta Ads.

Pillar 7 ensures those 200 inquiries are captured, organized, assigned, followed up, qualified, and tracked through the sales process so the business can identify how many eventually become customers.

One important operational principle: Advertising success should not be measured by impressions, clicks, or even lead volume alone. The business ultimately needs to understand whether its advertising is generating customers at an economically sustainable cost.
