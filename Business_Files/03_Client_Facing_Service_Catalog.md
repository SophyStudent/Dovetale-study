### Package 1: The Foundation Sprint (For Startups & New Ventures)
- **Target Audience:** Startups launching new products or services with zero existing infrastructure.
- **Core Objective:** Establish a clean, professional, measurable foundation to acquire initial customers without operational chaos.
- **Included Scope:**
  - Value proposition and audience profile definition.
  - High-converting core landing page or website.
  - Basic CRM setup with automated lead capture forms.
  - Essential tracking infrastructure: GA4, Meta Pixel / Google Tag, and verified form events.
  - Automated welcome sequence for inbound leads.
- **Business Outcome:** A fully functional, trackable system ready to handle inbound market traffic.

### Package 2: The Growth Engine (For Small Businesses)
- **Target Audience:** Established businesses (\$100k–\$1M+) seeking predictable customer acquisition and retention.
- **Core Objective:** Eliminate revenue leaks, improve website conversion rates, and build systematic follow-up.
- **Included Scope:**
  - Full marketing infrastructure diagnostic audit.
  - High-converting landing page suite (desktop and mobile optimized).
  - Lead capture routing, centralized CRM pipeline, and instant notification alerts.
  - Comprehensive email retention sequences (welcome, browse/cart abandonment, post-purchase).
  - Multi-channel attribution setup: GA4, GTM, Meta Conversions API (CAPI), and UTM standards.
  - Performance ad account structuring (Meta Ads and Google Ads).
- **Business Outcome:** Higher conversion rates, faster lead response times, and clear attribution on every advertising dollar.

### Package 3: Enterprise Integration & Optimization (For Medium Businesses)
- **Target Audience:** Scaling companies (\$1M–\$10M+) with multiple departments, complex sales cycles, or high transaction volumes.
- **Core Objective:** Unify fragmented software, automate manual processes, and provide executive-grade attribution modeling.
- **Included Scope:**
  - Deep-dive cross-platform audit (CRM, billing, marketing automation, ad platforms).
  - Custom data pipeline integration (via webhooks, APIs, Make, or Zapier).
  - Advanced lifecycle segmentation and RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) customer cohort modeling.
  - End-to-end attribution modeling connecting ad clicks to verified CRM sales and revenue.
  - Executive performance dashboards (Looker Studio or equivalent).
  - Ongoing CRO and system optimization retainer.
- **Business Outcome:** Automated operational workflows, eliminated manual data entry, and reliable revenue attribution across all channels.

## 4. Scope Boundaries & Technical Inclusions

### Included in Engagements:
- Full system architecture, technical configurations, software integrations, and measurement frameworks.
- Wireframing, conversion copywriting, technical tagging, and automation engineering.
- Strategic guidance on campaign structure, targeting parameters, and retention workflows.
- Comprehensive documentation and operational handover training.

### Excluded (Out of Scope):
- Daily organic social media posting or graphic-only vanity design.
- Traditional PR, offline print media buying, or influencer management.
- Custom software engineering unrelated to marketing/CRM stacks.
- Direct ad spend liabilities (all ad spend is billed directly to the client's merchant accounts).
