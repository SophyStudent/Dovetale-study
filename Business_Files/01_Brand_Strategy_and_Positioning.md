## 3. The Problem: The "Marketing Gap"

Most startups and SMEs treat marketing as a collection of isolated campaigns rather than a unified operational engine.

### Typical Disconnected Marketing Symptoms:
1. **Unattributed Ad Spend:** Running campaigns on Meta or Google without verified server-side tracking or conversion attribution. Management cannot identify which dollar yields which customer.
2. **Leaky Funnels:** Driving paid traffic to sluggish, generic landing pages where 97%+ of visitors drop off without converting.
3. **Siloed Lead Capture:** Inquiries arrive through disparate channels (WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, email, website forms) with no central CRM, resulting in missed leads and inconsistent follow-up.
4. **Zero Retention Mechanics:** Acquiring a customer once but lacking automated post-purchase onboarding, win-back flows, or lifecycle email marketing.
5. **Operational Drag:** Manual data entry between ad platforms, spreadsheets, and fulfillment, wasting hours weekly on repetitive tasks.

### The Dovetale Solution
Dovetale bridges this gap by engineering the underlying **marketing infrastructure**. We don't just launch campaigns; we build the pipes, tracking, data models, and automated pathways that ensure every marketing dollar is measurable, captured, and converted.
