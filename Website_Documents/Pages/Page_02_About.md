### Section 2: Why We Exist
- **Background:** White (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Headline (H2):** Why We Exist
- **Body Copy:**  
  We observed a consistent pattern across growing businesses:  
  
  Business owners and teams were working hard on marketing — running adverts, publishing content, maintaining websites and responding to customer enquiries.  
  
  Yet many felt uncertain about what was actually working. Their advertising platforms operated separately from their website analytics. Their enquiries arrived in different inboxes without a central record. Their customer data was scattered across spreadsheets, email tools and messaging apps.  
  
  The issue was rarely the business, the product or the effort invested. **The issue was the absence of a connected system.**  
  
  We created Dovetale to provide businesses with the structure, clarity and operational efficiency they need to market themselves effectively.

### Section 3: What We Believe
- **Background:** Light Grey (`#F4F6F8`)
- **Headline (H2):** What We Believe
- **Core Principles:**
  - **Marketing should be a connected system.** An advert, a landing page, an email sequence and a CRM are not separate services — they are components of a single customer journey. When they work together, every part performs better.
  - **Clarity comes before activity.** Before spending money on campaigns or building new tools, a business should understand its market, its customers, its performance data and its commercial objectives.
  - **Practical systems beat complex ones.** The best business systems are the ones your team can understand, manage and rely on every day. We prioritize practical functionality over unnecessary complexity.
  - **Decisions should be based on evidence.** You shouldn't have to guess whether your marketing is working. Reliable tracking and measurement give you the confidence to invest where it counts.
