## Section 2: The 9-Stage Implementation Journey

### 1. Initial Consultation
- **Focus:** Understanding your business, your current challenges and what you want to achieve.
- **What Happens:** We discuss your offerings, target audience, existing marketing activities, and the operational pain points holding you back. This helps us determine whether Dovetale is the right partner for your needs.

### 2. Business & Marketing Assessment
- **Focus:** Auditing your existing systems, processes, platforms and historical performance.
- **What Happens:** We examine your website, analytics, ad accounts, lead management process, email platforms, and customer data. We document what exists, evaluate what is working, and identify data reliability issues.

### 3. Strategy & Systems Planning
- **Focus:** Identifying gaps, commercial opportunities, and technical implementation requirements.
- **What Happens:** We develop a prioritized blueprint for your marketing infrastructure. We define which pillars need immediate attention, what tools should be configured, and how data will flow across your business.

### 4. Infrastructure Development
- **Focus:** Designing and constructing the required marketing systems.
- **What Happens:** We build the physical and digital assets: landing pages, CRM pipelines, conversion forms, tracking containers, and automated email sequences.

### 5. Integration & Implementation
- **Focus:** Connecting platforms, configuring tools, and establishing automated workflows.
- **What Happens:** We wire your platforms together using native connectors, webhooks, and automation engines. Data from website inquiries flows directly into your CRM, triggers email sequences, and syncs conversion signals to ad platforms.

### 6. Testing & Quality Assurance
- **Focus:** Checking functionality, tracking accuracy, and system reliability before launch.
- **What Happens:** We run thorough end-to-end tests: submitting test leads, triggering purchase events, inspecting tracking tags in GA4 DebugView, and validating email deliverability and mobile responsiveness.

### 7. Reporting & Handover
- **Focus:** Presenting completed work, comprehensive documentation, and operational guidance.
- **What Happens:** We walk your team through the completed infrastructure. You receive full administrative access, system architecture diagrams, and plain-English standard operating procedures (SOPs).

### 8. Ongoing Optimization
- **Focus:** Monitoring live performance, identifying improvements, and refining systems.
- **What Happens:** For clients on ongoing retainers, we analyze real-world customer interactions, run conversion rate optimization tests, tune ad campaign efficiency, and adapt workflows as your business scales.
